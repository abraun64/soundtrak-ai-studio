## Strategic spine
Audience: decision-makers in data centres, utilities, oil & gas — *plus* ABB's 110,000 employees as an internal advocacy audience. Insight: in a commoditised service market, buyers trust *people*, not service-line copy. Big idea: position expert service as a strategic differentiator by telling the stories of the people who deliver it. Channels: LinkedIn / YouTube / Google core, extended into Spotify and Connected TV, with multilingual localisation; internal push via town halls + leader comms.

## Creative move
**What it did**: authentic mini-docs of service professionals solving real customer problems, run externally *and* internally.

**The move**:
- *Protagonist swap* — the technician, not the service, is the hero.
- *Dual-audience engine* — the same content builds external trust and internal pride.
- *Format expansion* into audio + CTV to escape the LinkedIn-only B2B rut.
- *Sector-and-role segmentation* on paid.

**Why it worked**:
- Identifiable-hero effect — a face beats a spec sheet.
- Internal advocacy compounds external reach.
- Proof-through-people answers "can I trust this vendor?".

**When to steal**: commoditised B2B service; a workforce that IS the product; low-trust / high-consideration buy.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: staged "hero" stories ring false; neglecting the internal rollout wastes the advocacy dividend.
