## Strategic spine
Audience: business executives and enterprise decision-makers. Insight: the Fortune 500's 70th anniversary is a cultural asset — and a chance to demonstrate reinvention, not just talk about it. Big idea: reinvent the *list itself* with generative AI, so both Fortune and Accenture embody the innovation they sell. Anchored by a documentary brand film + a proprietary LLM trained on Fortune data, in a custom content hub.

## Creative move
**What it did**: paired a 10-minute documentary with a Fortune-data LLM that lets audiences query 70 years of enterprise history in natural language.

**The move**:
- *Prove it, don't pitch it* — demonstrate reinvention by reinventing an icon.
- *Data as an interactive product*, not a static report.
- *Borrowed authority* — co-brand with Fortune to inherit its credibility.
- *Film + tool + hub* — narrative sets meaning, the tool delivers utility.

**Why it worked**:
- A working genAI artefact is a stronger proof than a case study.
- Interactivity turns a passive milestone into engagement.
- The Fortune halo de-risks Accenture's innovation claim.

**When to steal**: a consultancy/tech brand that must *show* capability; access to a proprietary dataset; a cultural anniversary to hijack.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: AI-as-gimmick with no real utility; the partner brand overshadowing yours.
