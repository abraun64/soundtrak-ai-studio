## Strategic spine
Audience: international conference organisers and global business decision-makers choosing host cities. Insight: post-pandemic, delegates weigh purpose, sustainability, DEI and legacy — not just logistics. Big idea: reposition Sydney as a catalyst for change by proving depth in five high-growth sectors where the city leads. Each sector gets a gated e-book, three SEO articles and a tailored landing page, plus standalone depth pieces, built on 50+ expert interviews.

## Creative move
**What it did**: built five sector content ecosystems (tech/innovation, health/life-sciences, financial/professional services, science/engineering, aerospace/defence), each anchored by a gated e-book.

**The move**:
- *Sector segmentation* — one deep ecosystem per decision-maker type, not a generic brochure.
- *Gated e-book + SEO halo* — capture demand and rank for it.
- *Borrowed authority* — 50+ named experts (scientists, professors, leaders).
- *Reframe the offer* — sell purpose and legacy, not ballrooms.

**Why it worked**:
- Sector depth signals genuine capability to a specialist buyer.
- Gating converts high-intent research into leads.
- Purpose framing matches the post-pandemic decision criteria.

**When to steal**: a considered B2B choice with distinct buyer segments; a destination/place brand; a category where depth and credibility win.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: gating thin content kills trust; sector sprawl without genuine local proof.
