## Strategic spine
Audience: C-level executives and senior security/IT leaders at enterprises. Insight: the C-suite trusts peers, not vendor pitches. Big idea: an owned editorial platform authored by executives for executives, so Cloudflare builds mindshare and trust before the sale. Channel mix: organic, paid, owned and earned, with personalised web journeys keyed to topic interest.

## Creative move
**What it did**: runs a standing executive-grade publication rather than a blog, with content routed by reader interest into a deeper funnel.

**The move**:
- *"For C-suite, by C-suite"* — executive authors matched to target personas set the credibility bar.
- *Personalised web journeys* adapt the path by topic, not one-size-fits-all.
- *Publication, not campaign* — an always-on trust asset that compounds.

**Why it worked**:
- Peer authorship clears the trust bar a vendor voice can't.
- Personalisation lifts engagement and pulls readers toward sales stages.
- Owned media builds durable mindshare vs rented attention.

**When to steal**: high-consideration B2B where the buyer is senior and sceptical; a brand with genuine executive voices to deploy; a long sales cycle that rewards nurtured trust.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: "executive" content that's actually ghostwritten fluff; a publication with no distribution engine behind it.
