## Strategic spine
Audience: consumers worn out by restrictive New-Year wellness regimes. Insight: January guilt-culture is exhausting; permission to *not* overhaul yourself is the unmet want. Big idea: an "anti-challenge" that rewards existing greatness. Channel spine: SMS as the intimate, permission-based hero, amplified by social + influencers.

## Creative move
**What it did**: 30 days of daily SMS prompts nudging tiny acts of self-kindness (watch animal videos, take a real lunch break), with playful prizes and a UGC share loop.

**The move**:
- *Seasonal counter-position* — sell the opposite of the category's January script.
- *SMS as the relationship channel*, not a discount blast.
- *Micro-goals + delight prizes* (bread-scented candle, cozy robe) lower the participation bar.
- *UGC loop* turns participants into reach.

**Why it worked**:
- Reactance relief — opting out of pressure feels good.
- Tiny commitments beat big resolutions (behavioural friction).
- SMS intimacy drives completion and daily habit.

**When to steal**: a category with a tired seasonal ritual; a brand that can credibly grant "permission"; a product that slots into a daily habit.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: contrarianism with no product truth reads as a gimmick; SMS over-sending burns the opt-in.
