## Strategic spine
Audience: Albertans overwhelmed by a deregulated energy market. Insight: confusion is the real barrier, and static FAQs don't cut through it. Big idea: turn education into 10 interactive quizzes (energy habits, clean-tech decisions, reading your bill) with instant personalised results. Channels: social + email drive traffic; optional in-quiz forms capture leads; nurture emails follow up with tailored offers.

## Creative move
**What it did**: swapped a static help centre for a suite of playful, shareable quizzes that double as a lead engine.

**The move**:
- *Quiz-as-FAQ* — interactivity beats a wall of text.
- *Personalised result → tailored offer* bridges education to conversion.
- *Soft lead capture* (optional forms + prize incentive) keeps it low-friction.
- *Meme/GIF register* de-stiffens a dry utility category.

**Why it worked**:
- Active participation lifts recall vs passive reading.
- Personalised feedback feels useful, not salesy.
- Self-segmentation hands you a warm, qualified lead.

**When to steal**: a confusing/commoditised category; an education job with a conversion tail; a brand that needs first-party lead data.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: quizzes engineered only to harvest emails erode trust; results with no genuine utility feel like a bait-and-switch.
