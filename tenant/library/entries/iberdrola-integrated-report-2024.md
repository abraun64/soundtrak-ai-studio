> **Audience note:** corporate / investor communications rather than a marketing campaign — included as a craft reference for turning obligatory disclosure into engaging, accessible content.

## Strategic spine
Audience: investors, corporate stakeholders and sustainability-focused readers demanding transparency. Insight: a mandatory annual report is usually a dead PDF — but stakeholders now judge you on how clearly you disclose. Big idea: rebuild the integrated report as an interactive microsite that frames sustainability performance inside the growth strategy, not as a compliance appendix.

## Creative move
**What it did**: an interactive, bilingual microsite with dynamic charts, concise summaries and clear navigation across strategy, governance and sustainability metrics.

**The move**:
- *Report as product* — a navigable microsite, not a static download.
- *Data made legible* — dynamic charts + summary sections for mixed audiences.
- *Compliance as credibility* — CSRD / ISSB / GRI / TCFD alignment signals rigour.
- *Bilingual + accessible* — designed for a genuinely diverse stakeholder base.

**Why it worked**:
- Clarity and interactivity make dense disclosure actually get read.
- Framing sustainability within strategy elevates it from box-ticking.
- Standards compliance is the trust proof for a sceptical investor.

**When to steal**: any obligatory disclosure (annual/ESG/impact reports); an investor or regulator audience; a brand that wants transparency to read as strength.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: interactivity that buries the numbers; "sustainability comms" that outruns the actual performance (greenwashing risk).
