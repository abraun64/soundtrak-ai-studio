## Strategic spine
Audience: startup CEOs and operators. Insight: founders won't read a data dump, but they'll watch a two-minute story that respects their time. Big idea: translate the quarterly Sōzō CEO survey into an animated, illustrated narrative where every metric becomes a visual metaphor — data-driven yet founder-friendly, matching the brand's editorial identity.

## Creative move
**What it did**: a two-minute animated film turning survey findings into custom illustration + motion graphics with an original soundtrack.

**The move**:
- *Metaphor per data point* — a factory rebooting for operational resurgence; clearing weather for the economic outlook.
- *Personify the numbers* — give abstract data a character and motion.
- *Format for the feed* — optimised for social + newsletter embedding.
- *Consistent editorial identity* — visual system reinforces the Sōzō brand.

**Why it worked**:
- Visual metaphor makes abstract macro data memorable and shareable.
- Short, designed motion respects a founder's attention.
- A recognisable visual system compounds brand equity issue over issue.

**When to steal**: recurring proprietary data (surveys, indices, reports); a time-poor senior audience; a brand that wants a distinctive visual signature.

## Visual direction
Custom hand-illustrated frames with motion-graphic transitions and visual metaphors; each frame designed to carry a single idea. A strong reference for **data-to-illustration** editorial work.
