## Strategic spine
Audience: small and mid-sized insulation contractors with no in-house marketing. Insight: the 25C federal tax credit is a huge demand lever, but contractors can't translate policy into customer outreach. Big idea: NAIMA does the marketing *for* them — free, personalised, ready to ship — so the whole channel can sell the credit.

## Creative move
**What it did**: built a customisable toolkit + portal that auto-generates thousands of branded assets personalised to each contractor.

**The move**:
- *Do the work for the channel* — remove the execution barrier, not just the knowledge gap.
- *Personalisation at scale* — auto-brand assets with each contractor's name, service area and links.
- *Full-funnel toolkit* — landing pages, video, email, infographics, sales training, sample invoices.
- *Free* — the association absorbs the cost to move the category.

**Why it worked**:
- Turns a policy incentive into shippable contractor demand-gen.
- Scales 1:many while feeling 1:1 to each contractor.
- Association-funded generosity builds durable channel loyalty.

**When to steal**: a brand/association whose growth depends on under-resourced partners; a complex incentive that needs translating into outreach; channel or co-op marketing.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: generic co-branded content partners won't use; a toolkit with no adoption support.
