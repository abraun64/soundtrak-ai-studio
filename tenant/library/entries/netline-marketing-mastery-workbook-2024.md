## Strategic spine
Audience: B2B marketers, demand-gen leaders, content strategists, sales-enablement teams. Insight: B2B marketers are drowning in theory but starved of usable execution tools under compressed timelines. Big idea: an operational *workbook* — not a whitepaper — that turns NetLine's proprietary buyer-intent data into actionable frameworks (targeting, content, campaigns) marketers can apply immediately.

## Creative move
**What it did**: built an interactive, tactile field guide with worksheets, cheat sheets and "Mistakes to Avoid," distributed physically at INBOUND, Content Marketing World and Forrester's B2B Summit.

**The move**:
- *Workbook, not whitepaper* — designed for *doing*, not just reading.
- *Proprietary data as the moat* — grounded in intent data no one else has.
- *Honest, direct tone* with personality ("Mistakes to Avoid").
- *Physical presence at events* — a tangible flagship that starts conversations.

**Why it worked**:
- Utility drives use, sharing and recall far beyond a gated PDF.
- Proprietary data earns credibility a generic guide can't.
- A tactile object at events converts attention into pipeline.

**When to steal**: a B2B brand sitting on proprietary data; an audience that wants execution tools; an event-led motion.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: a "workbook" that's a rebadged brochure; interactivity with no genuine utility.
