> **Audience note:** queued as a "B2B" award but Puck sells to individual subscribers — this is **B2C / subscription-media** content. Filed accordingly; the participatory mechanic is the transferable value.

## Strategic spine
Audience: engaged, opinionated political-news subscribers. Insight: in a saturated election cycle, exclusive *participation* is worth more than more coverage. Big idea: survey readers, then build the editorial answer *around their questions* in a premium interactive format — so subscribers see themselves in the product.

## Creative move
**What it did**: ran a reader survey, then answered top questions in a magazine-style scrolling Q&A with animation, illustration and pull quotes that evolved as new questions came in.

**The move**:
- *Audience input as the brief* — the content is co-authored by subscribers.
- *Premium interactive template* — anchored navigation, embedded media, motion.
- *Exclusivity* — reserved for paying subscribers, reinforcing the value of the tier.
- *Living document* — dialogue updates as questions emerge.

**Why it worked**:
- Being heard deepens loyalty and belonging.
- A premium format differentiates in a commoditised news moment.
- Subscriber-only access reinforces the paid identity.

**When to steal**: a subscription/community brand; a moment of category saturation; an audience with strong opinions worth surfacing.

**Anti-patterns / risks**: soliciting input then ignoring it; interactivity that's decorative rather than substantive.
